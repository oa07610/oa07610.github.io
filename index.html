I am a master's student in Natural Language Processing at MBZUAI, advised by Preslav Nakov. I am currently a visiting researcher at Macquarie University in Sydney, working with Usman Naseem on cultural alignment in language models. Before MBZUAI, I studied computer science at Habib University in Karachi.

My research is about the reliability of large language models: what a model actually knows, when its answer is supported by that knowledge or by what it retrieves, and how to tell the difference. I am most interested in these questions in multilingual and underrepresented settings, such as Arabic, Urdu and other Pakistani languages, where the failures are easiest to see. I also work on interpretability of how models represent this knowledge, and on evaluation methodology, including benchmark construction and contamination.

I am applying to PhD programs for Fall 2027.

Research Experience

Macquarie University, Sydney Sep 2026 – present
Visiting Researcher, School of Computing, with Usman Naseem

Cultural alignment in language models: whose norms a model reproduces when it answers in a given language, and where in the model that choice is made. Leading KINSHIFT, a kinship-reasoning benchmark for Urdu, Sindhi, Punjabi and Pashto in native and Roman scripts, and extending it to localization and steering of the responsible model components. First-author paper in preparation.

Institute of Foundation Models, MBZUAI Jun 2026 – Aug 2026
Research Intern, Jais-3 Data Team

Mapped the knowledge boundaries of five open LLMs (8B–120B) over a 791K-fact Arabic/English bank, and built a fine-tuning set restricted to facts the model already knew. Also designed verifiable-reward RL tasks for Arabic morphology, i'rab and poetry, and built bilingual agentic search trajectories grounded in retrieved documents. First author on the resulting COLM 2026 workshop paper. Co-author of the Jais 3 technical report.

MBZUAI, Abu Dhabi Aug 2025 – present
Graduate Researcher, advised by Preslav Nakov

Retrieval-augmented generation and long-context QA. Showed that failures usually attributed to reading are driven mainly by retrieval coverage, and built QIMG-7, a paired clean/polluted benchmark showing that polluted retrieved text is the main failure channel in multimodal RAG. Two EMNLP 2026 papers, co-first author on both. Contributed to UrduMMLU, a natively authored Urdu benchmark of 26,389 questions.
